I'm very happy to share that our lab has been awarded funding as part of a new European research consortium! The official announcement will come later, but I can already share a few details about the project and, most importantly, 
start looking for people to join us!

The project will investigate **how exposure to nanoplastics during pregnancy may affect early brain development**. 
Can these tiny particles reach the developing brain? And if so, what are their consequences for the maturation of neuronal circuits and brain function? 
Together with a unique multidisciplinary team of collaborators, we will address these questions by combining different experimental approaches, from animal models to human brain organoids.

**And this brings me to the exciting part: we are recruiting!**

I'm looking for **one postdoc (2 years) and one PhD student (4 years, fully funded)** to join our lab at the GIGA Institute, ideally starting in March 2027.

If you're interested in developmental neuroscience and would like to work on a project combining fundamental research with a question of growing environmental and public health relevance, I'd love to hear from you!

Feel free to [get in touch](mailto:mchini@uliege.be?subject=European%20consortium%20research%20position), and please share with anyone who might be interested!
